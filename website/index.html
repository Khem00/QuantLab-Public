THE QUANTLAB PRINCIPLE

AI-assisted.
Evidence-governed.

Automation can help build and examine research workflows. It does not make a result reliable by itself.

QuantLab emphasizes explicit contracts, deterministic execution, regression tests, chronological validation, transparent assumptions, and human review. A completed run is not proof of future performance.

A REAL RESEARCH EXAMPLE

BTC #001: a measured conclusion.

A frozen SMA20/SMA50 rule was evaluated against buy-and-hold on a chronological out-of-sample period. The conclusion is deliberately nuanced.

OOS total return42.13%Strategy · vs 45.79% buy-and-hold
Maximum drawdown−22.21%Strategy · vs −31.84% buy-and-hold
Annualized volatility14.52%Strategy · vs 16.43% buy-and-hold
Sharpe ratio0.5263Strategy · vs 0.5124 buy-and-hold
!
CAUTION — drawdown mitigation evidence, not superior returns.

The strategy lagged buy-and-hold in cumulative return while experiencing lower historical drawdown and volatility. This is evidence about the tested period and assumptions, not proof of alpha or future performance.
