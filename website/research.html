METHODOLOGY

Freeze first. Evaluate later.

01 / Strategy

Long when SMA20 is above SMA50; otherwise flat. Parameters were frozen before the OOS evaluation.

02 / Split

70% development period and chronological holdout. No random shuffling. Fifty warm-up bars retained for rolling indicators.

03 / Execution

One-bar execution convention, initial capital 100,000, position size 1.0, base commission and slippage set to zero.

04 / Evaluation

Fresh OOS portfolio at the boundary. Pre-OOS signals neutralized. Benchmark: buy-and-hold.

LIMITATIONS

What this study does not establish.

  • It does not establish future or live performance.
  • It does not establish robustness across other assets, venues, or market regimes.
  • Base commission and slippage were zero; the base result does not represent complete fee modelling.
  • It does not establish statistical significance or investability.
  • Historical data quality, provenance, and licensing remain relevant to interpretation.

This research is informational and methodological. It is not investment advice, a trading signal, or a recommendation to buy or sell any asset.
