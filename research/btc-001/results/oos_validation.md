# OOS Validation

## Result

**CAUTION — no return superiority demonstrated.**

| Metric | SMA20/SMA50 | Buy & Hold | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Total return | 42.13% | 45.79% | -3.66 pp |
| Annualized return | 6.81% | 7.32% | — |
| Sharpe ratio | 0.5263 | 0.5124 | +0.0139 |
| Maximum drawdown | -22.21% | -31.84% | +9.63 pp |
| Volatility | 14.52% | 16.43% | -1.91 pp |

The strategy therefore traded some cumulative return for lower historical drawdown and volatility.

The slightly higher Sharpe ratio is a numerical observation, not evidence of statistically significant outperformance.

## Evaluation period

OOS evaluation covers 1,295 observations from 2022-12-23 through 2026-07-09.

The underlying machine-readable results are:

- `evidence/validation/oos_summary.csv`
- `evidence/validation/sma_cross_oos_equity_curve.csv`
- `evidence/validation/buy_hold_oos_equity_curve.csv`

## Interpretation

The evidence supports a risk/exposure-management interpretation rather than a claim of superior return generation.
