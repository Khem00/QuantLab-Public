# Exposure Analysis

During the OOS period:

- Observations: 1,295
- Invested days: 744
- Flat days: 551
- BTC exposure: 57.45%
- Flat: 42.55%
- Entries: 17
- Exits: 17
- Completed trades: 17
- Open position at OOS end: No

The strategy was therefore invested for approximately 57% of the OOS observations and flat for approximately 43%.

This reduced exposure is a key part of the mechanism behind the lower maximum drawdown.

The strategy's return while invested was 34.99%, while buy-and-hold's return during the strategy's flat days was 14.62%.

These figures should not be interpreted as a decomposition of causal alpha. They describe the observed exposure pattern.

The machine-readable evidence is in:

- `evidence/exposure/exposure_summary.csv`
- `evidence/exposure/position_events.csv`

The overall conclusion remains that the strategy is better interpreted as an exposure-management rule than as a demonstrated return-maximizing alpha strategy.
